# MOSUL-GENESIS-000001 — buyer terms

**STATUS: DRAFT — CHECKOUT MUST REMAIN DISABLED UNTIL SIGNED**

The final signed version must identify the legal seller, country/jurisdiction,
support contact, exact product, price, delivery window, refund rule, governing
law, dispute contact, and permitted use. Its exact SHA-256 must replace the
placeholder in the product configuration and storefront metadata.

Minimum product disclosure:

- One digital Collector Master, certificate, and verification records.
- Edition 1/1 applies to this issued product identity; it does not make the
  underlying 1932 public-domain source exclusive.
- Personal display and archival use only unless the signed version explicitly
  grants broader commercial rights.
- No copyright, trademark, token, investment, resale-value, or NFT promise.
- The NFT field remains not included unless a successful mint and asset address
  are published before checkout.
- Payment is final only after independent verification of the exact finalized
  Solana Mainnet USDC transfer.
- The signed terms must state whether a refund is available before download,
  after download, when delivery fails, and any non-waivable buyer rights.

Do not set SELLER_TERMS_ACCEPTED=1 while this draft marker remains.
