## Decision

Set the opening bid at **100 USDC**, with **no hidden reserve**, and the minimum
increment at **10 USDC**. The opening is intentionally well below the two
completed regional digital-art results because this project has no prior sales,
gallery representation or tokenized secondary market. A 10% first increment
is easy to understand and broadly consistent with traditional auction-step
practice. Competition, if any, determines the final price.

Review this setting after the first completed sale. Future recommendations must
use only verified completed sales, never asking prices, and must disclose the
sample size and material differences.

## Contract design sources

The project did not copy another platform's contract. It adapted recurring
buyer-protection and auction-integrity concepts from Christie's Dubai online
conditions and Sotheby's buyer conditions: terms accepted by bidding, clear
seller/lot identity, binding bids, hammer-price definition, payment obligations,
intellectual-property limits, technical-risk disclosure, written notices,
severability and governing law. Iraqi mandatory law controls this direct sale.

- Christie's Dubai online-only conditions (1 Jan 2024):
  https://www.christies.com/-/media/conditions-of-sale/EcommerceConditionsOfSale-Dubai-1Jan24.pdf
- Sotheby's buyer conditions:
  https://www.sothebys.com/conditions-of-business-for-buyers-london-02.07.2024.pdf
- Iraqi Electronic Signatures and Transactions Law No. 78 of 2012 (official
  Gazette listing): https://moj.gov.iq/view.205/

The wallet-signature process is designed to identify the signing wallet,
capture agreement, detect any terms change through the hash, and retain the
message/signature/time. It is cryptographic evidence, not a claim of an
accredited authentication certificate under Iraqi law.
